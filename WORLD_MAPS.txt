================================================================================
          THE SIMPSONS: HIT & RUN - WORLD MAPS
================================================================================

This file contains ASCII maps of the three unique world layouts used in the game.
Coordinates are based on teleport destinations from level scripts.

Note: Levels reuse maps with different missions/time of day:
  - Suburban Springfield: Levels 1, 4, 7 (Halloween)
  - Downtown Springfield: Levels 2, 5
  - Entertainment District: Levels 3, 6
